Introduction

I BUILD DIGITAL EXPERIENCES WHERE CODE, 3D AND MOTION MEET.

Practice

I design and build digital products end-to-end: UI, motion and real-time 3D, then ship them myself in code.

A lot of this work started as a question. How a scroll should feel when it carries weight. Whether a product can be lit convincingly without ever being photographed. What a tool becomes when its 3D runs live instead of rendered. Building it was the only way to find out.

About me

Today I build platforms and software in Next.js, TypeScript and Three.js, alongside ongoing work as a digital artist across illustration, comics and NFT projects.
